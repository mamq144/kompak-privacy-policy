Core Principle of KomPak: KomPak is engineered to operate without external cloud servers. All compression, optimization, and processing of images, videos, and PDFs happen 100% locally on your device hardware and never leave your phone.

1. Overview & Zero Data Collection

This Privacy Policy explains how KomPak (developed by Mohammed Alqahtani) handles user data. We strongly believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Therefore, KomPak is built upon the foundation of Zero Data Collection.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Your Files and Media: Photos, videos, and PDFs selected for compression are processed exclusively by your device's local CPU/GPU. We do not inspect, upload, or transmit your media to any cloud servers.
  • Personal Information: No account creation is required. We never ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, or identity.
  • Location Data: We never request or track your geographic location.
  • Contacts & Sensitive Info: We have zero access to your contacts, address book, or system logs.

3. Permissions Required

KomPak uses the minimum system permissions necessary to perform local functions:

  • Photo & Media Library: To enable selecting media files for compression and saving optimized copies back to your device Gallery / Camera Roll.
