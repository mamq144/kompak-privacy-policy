🛡️ 100% Privacy-First ⚡ 100% On-Device Offline Processing Last Updated: September 30, 2026
Core Principle of KomPak: KomPak is engineered to operate without external cloud servers. All compression, optimization, and processing of images, videos, and media happen entirely on your device and never leave your phone.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how KomPak (developed by Mohammed Alqhtani) handles user data. We strongly believe privacy is a fundamental right; therefore, KomPak is built upon the principle of Zero Data Collection.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Your Files and Media: Photos, videos, and audio files selected for compression are processed exclusively by your device's local processor. We do not access, inspect, or upload your media to any cloud servers.
  • Personal Information: No account creation is required. We do not ask for or collect names, emails, phone numbers, or identities.
  • Location Data: We never request or track your geographic location.
  • Contacts & Sensitive Info: We do not request or have access to your contacts or device logs.

3. Permissions Required

KomPak requests only the minimal permissions essential to its core local functions:

  • Photo & Media Library: To enable selecting media files for compression and saving the compressed copies back to your gallery.
  • Camera (Optional): If you choose to capture a new photo or video directly inside the app for immediate compression.
  • Storage: To read and write compressed files locally on your device.

4. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

KomPak offers optional premium features:

  • Lifetime Purchase (Non-Consumable): A one-time purchase unlocking all pro features permanently.
  • Annual Subscription (Auto-Renewable): Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing cycle.

All financial transactions are handled securely and directly by Google Play Billing on Android and Apple App Store In-App Purchases on iOS. The developer never accesses, processes, or stores your financial or payment details.

5. Security

Because all compression takes place locally within the app's sandboxed environment, your data security is safeguarded by the built-in operating system security of iOS and Android.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal data from children under 13, adhering to COPPA regulations and Google Play / Apple Family Policies.
